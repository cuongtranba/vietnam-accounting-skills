Dieu 31 ND 214/2025/ND-CP - hai chuan don gia NGUOC NHAU, rat de nham:
1) Diem c khoan 2 (hieu chinh sai lech de SO SANH, XEP HANG): hang muc chao thieu ma HSDT khong co don gia thi lay DON GIA CHAO CAO NHAT cua hang muc do trong so cac HSDT khac da vuot qua buoc danh gia ve ky thuat; neu khong co thi lay don gia trong du toan goi thau; neu khong co du toan thi lay don gia hinh thanh gia goi thau.
2) Khoan 4 (xac dinh GIA DE NGHI TRUNG THAU cho nha thau xep hang nhat): lay DON GIA THAP NHAT trong cac HSDT vuot qua buoc ky thuat, roi den du toan, roi den gia goi thau.
=> Cao nhat khi xep hang, thap nhat khi ap gia trung thau.
LUU Y: references/dau-thau.md cua skill va script so_sanh_thau.py (bien don_gia_thap_nhat, dong ~168-176) dang dung DON GIA THAP NHAT cho buoc hieu chinh xep hang -> NGUOC voi diem c khoan 2. Khi dung script phai tu tinh lai phan nay.
Khoan 1: loi so hoc - khong nhat quan giua don gia va thanh tien thi LAY DON GIA lam co so sua loi; chi khi don gia sai khac bat thuong do loi he thap phan (10/100/1.000 lan) thi lay thanh tien lam co so.
Khoan 3: ty le % sai lech thieu xac dinh so voi GIA DU THAU GHI TRONG DON DU THAU (chua tru giam gia).
Nguong 10%: dieu kien xet duyet trung thau nam o diem d khoan 1 Dieu 61 Luat Dau thau 22/2023/QH15 (da sua doi 2024-2025) - 'gia tri phan sai lech thieu khong qua 10% gia du thau' - KHONG nam trong Dieu 31 ND 214.
