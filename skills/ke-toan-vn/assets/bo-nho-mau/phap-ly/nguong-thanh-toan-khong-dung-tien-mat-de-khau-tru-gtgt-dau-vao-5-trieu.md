Tu 01/07/2025, nguong bat buoc phai co chung tu thanh toan khong dung tien mat de duoc khau tru thue GTGT dau vao ha tu TREN 20 trieu xuong TU 5 TRIEU tro len. Can cu: diem b khoan 2 Dieu 14 Luat GTGT 48/2024/QH15, huong dan tai khoan 2 Dieu 26 ND 181/2025/ND-CP (ban hanh 30/06/2025). Nguong 5 trieu DA BAO GOM thue GTGT. Chong chia nho hoa don: mua nhieu lan trong CUNG MOT NGAY tu cung mot nha cung cap, cong lai tu 5 trieu tro len thi cung phai co chung tu khong dung tien mat. Chung tu hop le theo ND 52/2024/ND-CP: chuyen khoan, the ngan hang, vi dien tu, bu tru cong no, thanh toan qua trung gian; TRU truong hop ben mua nop tien mat vao tai khoan ben ban. Canh bao thoi quen nghe: rat nhieu tai lieu cu va anh em ke toan van noi 20 trieu - da SAI ke tu 01/07/2025. Anh huong ca chi phi duoc tru khi tinh thue TNDN.
